# AI Tools for Job Seekers

Practical, responsible AI workflows that help job seekers understand opportunities, prepare for interviews, and organize their search.

## Available tools

| Tool | What it helps with |
|---|---|
| [AI Job Description Decoder](job-description-decoder.md) | Separates core requirements from preferences and connects a job seeker’s real experience to the role |
| [AI STAR Interview Story Builder](star-interview-story-builder.md) | Turns factual experience into clear behavioral interview stories without inventing accomplishments |
| [AI Job Search Pipeline Organizer](job-search-pipeline-organizer.md) | Organizes application updates, interview stages, follow-ups, deadlines, and next actions |

## How to use these resources

1. Remove personal, private, and confidential information.
2. Copy the appropriate prompt into an AI assistant.
3. Provide only factual information you are comfortable sharing.
4. Review every recommendation for accuracy.
5. Keep final applications, communications, and career decisions under your control.

## Responsible AI principles

- Never fabricate qualifications, accomplishments, interviews, or employer feedback.
- Do not treat AI output as an employment prediction.
- Protect personal contact information, private links, interview codes, references, and identity documents.
- Verify every date, deadline, stage, and claim.
- Keep the final content in your own voice.
