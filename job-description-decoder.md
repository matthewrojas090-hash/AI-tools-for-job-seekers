# AI Job Description Decoder

Turn a complicated job posting into a clear application strategy—without exaggerating or inventing qualifications.

## What this tool does

This AI prompt helps a job seeker:

- Separate required qualifications from preferred qualifications
- Identify the employer’s most important needs
- Translate job-posting language into plain English
- Find relevant experience to emphasize
- Identify honest experience gaps
- Generate interview-preparation topics
- Avoid wasting time tailoring a resume around minor keywords

## What you need

1. The complete job description
2. Your resume or a factual summary of your experience
3. An AI assistant such as ChatGPT

Remove your address, phone number, references, and other sensitive information before uploading your resume.

## Copy-and-paste prompt

Replace the bracketed sections with your information.

---

You are helping me understand a job description and prepare an honest, targeted application.

### Job description

[PASTE THE COMPLETE JOB DESCRIPTION]

### My experience

[PASTE YOUR RESUME OR A FACTUAL SUMMARY OF YOUR EXPERIENCE]

Analyze the information using only the material I provided. Do not invent qualifications, accomplishments, employers, education, certifications, or metrics.

Create the following:

1. **Role summary:** Explain the position in five clear bullet points.
2. **Top priorities:** Identify the five problems the employer most likely needs this person to solve.
3. **Required qualifications:** List the qualifications presented as essential.
4. **Preferred qualifications:** List qualifications that appear helpful but not mandatory.
5. **My strongest matches:** Connect my actual experience to the role and cite the supporting evidence.
6. **Transferable experience:** Identify relevant experience that may use different terminology.
7. **Possible gaps:** Flag requirements that are missing or unclear in my background. Do not assume I possess them.
8. **Resume priorities:** Recommend the five experiences or accomplishments I should emphasize.
9. **Keywords:** Provide 10 to 15 relevant terms that appear naturally supported by my experience.
10. **Interview preparation:** Suggest five likely interview questions based on the job description.
11. **Clarifying questions:** Ask me up to five questions that could uncover additional truthful experience before I tailor my application.

Finish with this reminder: “Verify every recommendation before using it. Never add a qualification or result you cannot support.”

## Fictional example

### Job description excerpt

> Northstar Labs is seeking a Recruiting Operations Manager to improve recruiting processes, reporting, interviewer training, and candidate experience. The successful candidate will have experience with an applicant tracking system, recruiting metrics, cross-functional projects, and process improvement. Experience managing a team is preferred.

### Fictional candidate experience

> Jordan Lee has six years of recruiting experience. Jordan created weekly hiring reports, trained 25 interviewers, documented recruiting procedures, and coordinated projects between recruiting, HR, and business leaders. Jordan used Greenhouse but has not formally managed employees.

### Example AI analysis

**Strongest matches**

- Recruiting reporting: Jordan created weekly hiring reports.
- Interviewer training: Jordan trained 25 interviewers.
- Process documentation: Jordan developed recruiting procedures.
- Cross-functional work: Jordan coordinated projects across recruiting, HR, and business teams.
- ATS experience: Jordan worked in Greenhouse.

**Possible gap**

- Formal people management is preferred, but Jordan’s background does not confirm direct-report responsibility.
- Jordan should not claim management experience unless additional factual evidence supports it.
- Jordan can still emphasize training, coordination, and project leadership.

**Resume priorities**

1. Quantify the impact of the weekly hiring reports.
2. Highlight the 25 interviewers trained.
3. Describe the recruiting procedures created.
4. Explain the cross-functional projects coordinated.
5. Include Greenhouse experience in the skills section.

## How to review the AI output

Before using the recommendations, ask:

- Is every claim supported by my real experience?
- Did the AI mistake a preference for a requirement?
- Did it add skills, results, or responsibilities I never provided?
- Do the suggested keywords sound natural?
- Can I explain every resume statement during an interview?

## Responsible-use guidelines

- Never use AI to fabricate experience.
- Do not copy confidential employer information into a public AI system.
- Treat the analysis as guidance—not a hiring prediction.
- Do not rely on an invented “ATS score.”
- Keep your final resume and application in your own voice.
- Review every output for accuracy before submitting it.

## Final reminder

A strong application does not need to match every preferred qualification. Focus on the employer’s central needs, demonstrate relevant evidence, and address genuine gaps honestly.
