# AI Resume Evidence Builder

Turn rough notes about work you actually did into clear resume bullets that you can explain and defend in an interview.

## What this tool does

A resume bullet should show more than a responsibility. It should help a reader understand the problem, your contribution, and the result. But job seekers do not always have a neat metric for every accomplishment.

This workflow helps you:

- Identify the strongest evidence in your experience
- Separate your contribution from the team's work
- Improve vague or task-only resume bullets
- Connect relevant experience to a specific job
- Describe results accurately, with or without numbers
- Spot claims that need verification
- Prepare examples you can discuss in an interview

AI helps draft and organize. You decide what is accurate and what belongs on your resume.

## Before you begin

Gather the job description and factual notes about two or three relevant projects or responsibilities. Remove personal contact details and confidential employer, customer, employee, or candidate information before using an external AI tool.

Use `Unknown` where you cannot confirm a detail. An estimate is not a verified result.

## Experience-notes template

Complete one copy for each experience you want to consider:

```text
Role and employer, or a general description if confidential:
Time period, if relevant:

What problem, goal, or recurring need did you address?
What was your specific responsibility?
What did you personally do?
What did teammates or partners do?
Which tools, methods, or skills did you use?
What changed as a result?
Which numbers or outcomes can you verify?
What feedback or other nonnumeric evidence can you verify?
What is still unclear?
What information must remain confidential?

My current resume bullet, if I have one:
```

## Copy-and-paste AI prompt

```text
Help me improve my resume using only the factual information below.

Do not invent a metric, outcome, employer, title, tool, scope, degree, certification, or responsibility. Do not turn a team achievement into my individual achievement. Do not calculate a percentage unless I supplied verified numbers that support it. If a fact is missing, label it "Needs confirmation."

Target job description:
[PASTE THE JOB DESCRIPTION]

My experience notes:
[PASTE ONE OR MORE COMPLETED TEMPLATES]

My current resume bullets, if any:
[PASTE THE BULLETS]

Complete these steps:

1. Evidence inventory
Create a table with these columns:
| Experience | Verified action | Verified result | My contribution | Relevant job requirement | Missing evidence |

Quote or briefly identify the information in my notes supporting each connection. If a requirement is not supported, say so.

2. Claim check
Flag any existing or proposed bullet that:
- Implies I owned work I only supported
- Uses an unverified number or result
- Changes a preference in the job description into a claimed qualification
- Uses vague language such as "responsible for" when a specific action is available
- Reveals confidential information
- Sounds more senior or technically deep than the evidence supports

3. Bullet drafts
For each experience, write up to three alternatives:
- Results-first: lead with a verified outcome, if one exists
- Action-first: lead with my specific contribution
- Scope-first: lead with the scale or complexity, only if verified

Keep each bullet concise and natural. Use the job description's terminology only where it accurately describes my experience. Do not force keywords into a bullet.

If there is no verified metric, write a strong qualitative bullet using supported actions and outcomes. Do not insert a placeholder number into a final-ready bullet.

4. Fit review
Rank the drafted bullets by relevance to the target role, using the job description and my supplied evidence. Explain the ranking. Do not predict whether I will be interviewed or hired.

5. Questions for me
Ask up to five focused questions that could uncover stronger truthful evidence, such as the scale of work, my decisions, feedback received, or an outcome I can verify. Do not suggest answers for me.

6. Interview check
For each recommended bullet, give me one follow-up question an interviewer might ask to verify my contribution or result.

Finish with:
"Use only bullets you can verify and explain in your own words."
```

## Fictional example

### Rough notes

```text
Role: Recruiting Operations Coordinator at Northstar Labs
Problem: Interviewers sometimes submitted feedback late.
My contribution: I reviewed the existing reminder process, created a standard
schedule for reminders, and wrote a short feedback guide.
Team contribution: The recruiting manager approved the process and introduced
it to interviewers.
Verified result: On-time feedback rose from 62% to 84% over four weeks,
according to the team's tracking report.
Confidential information: Individual interviewer records must remain private.
Current bullet: Responsible for interview feedback and process improvements.
```

### Improved bullet

> Created a consistent interviewer reminder process and feedback guide, contributing to an increase in on-time feedback from 62% to 84% over four weeks.

This wording names Jordan's actions and a verified team result without claiming Jordan independently caused the entire improvement or managed the interviewers.

### If the result had not been verified

> Created a consistent interviewer reminder process and feedback guide to help the recruiting team track feedback and identify delays.

That version is less specific, but it does not invent a metric.

### Interview follow-up

> How did you identify the problem, what part of the new process did you personally create, and how was the change measured?

Every person, organization, and number in this example is fictional.

## Final review

Before copying a bullet to your resume, confirm:

- Did I actually do what the bullet says?
- Is my individual contribution clear?
- Can I show where every number came from?
- Does the result reflect what the evidence establishes?
- Would a teammate describe my role the same way?
- Can I answer an interviewer's follow-up question?
- Did I remove confidential information?
- Does this sound like me?

## Responsible use and privacy

- Do not upload private employer documents, candidate records, customer details, or unpublished business results to an unapproved AI system.
- Remove your address, phone number, references, and unnecessary identifiers.
- Verify dates, titles, tools, numbers, and outcomes against your own records.
- Do not use AI to fill gaps with plausible-sounding accomplishments.
- Do not treat an AI-generated relevance ranking as an applicant tracking system score or hiring prediction.
- Keep your final resume in your own voice.

## Intended impact

This workflow is designed to make resume tailoring clearer and less time-consuming. Its value depends on the quality of your notes and your review of the drafts. It does not guarantee interviews or employment.
